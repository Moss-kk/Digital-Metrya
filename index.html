Features & Services

More than just invitations

Everything you need to plan, share and remember your event, in one place.

🎨

Custom designs

Designs shaped around your event and your personal story. We don't just paste details — we tailor colors and typography to your taste.

📊

RSVP tracking

See who is coming, as the answers arrive in real time. Download your guest list or receive instant notifications on Telegram.

📲

Guest check-in

Scan a QR code for smooth, VIP entry on the big day. Perfect for reserved seating and avoiding gate confusion.

📸

Photo and video sharing

Let guests add their own candid moments to your community gallery before, during, and after the celebration.

📍

Event details

Schedule, Google Maps directions, dress code, Ethiopian calendar dates, and everything else your guests ask.

🤝

Ongoing support

We are with you at every step, before and after the day. Update time or venue with one message and all links refresh instantly.

Choose a template
