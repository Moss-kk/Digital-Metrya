Common questions

Got questions?

What is a digital invitation?▾
A digital invitation is a beautiful web page designed specifically for your event. Guests open it by tapping a link or scanning a QR code — no app download needed. It includes event details, photos, map, countdown, and RSVP.
Do my guests need to install anything?▾
No. The invitation opens directly in any web browser on Android, iPhone, or any other phone. Just tap the link.
How long does it take?▾
Simple is ready in 3 days. Signature in 5 days. Rush delivery is available — ask us on Telegram and we'll do our best to accommodate.
Can I update details after it goes live?▾
Yes — that's one of the biggest advantages over printed cards. If your venue changes or timing shifts, we update the page and all existing links automatically show the new details.
Can you do Amharic and Ethiopian calendar dates?▾
Absolutely. We support bilingual English + Amharic invitations with the Ethiopian calendar date alongside the Gregorian date. Noto Sans Ethiopic is used for clean Ethiopic script.
What about guests who don't use smartphones?▾
We give you a print-ready QR code to put on a physical card — older relatives still receive a beautiful card, and the QR takes them to the full digital invitation. You can also share the link by SMS.
How do I pay?▾
We accept Telebirr, CBE Birr, and bank transfer. Payment details are shared once we confirm your order on Telegram. No payment is required upfront — we confirm all details first.
