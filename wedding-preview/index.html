Our Story

A Love Written in Stars

What began as a chance meeting in Addis Ababa grew into something neither of us could have imagined. Tamirat and Deborah's journey is one of laughter, shared dreams, and unwavering devotion. Through every season, through the highs and the quiet ordinary moments, our love only deepened.

"Love is not about finding the perfect person, but seeing an imperfect person perfectly."

Today, we stand at the threshold of a new chapter — and we could not imagine beginning it without the people who have shaped us, loved us, and walked with us. We invite you to witness our vows and celebrate this beautiful union with us.
