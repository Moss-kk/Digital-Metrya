# 01 · Brand System (source: Digital Metrya Brand Guidelines v1)

Always use this. Never improvise colors, fonts or logo treatment.

## Identity
- **Name:** Digital Metrya
- **Tagline:** Your Event. Our Digital Touch.
- **Flow:** Ideas → Digital → Memories
- **Purpose:** Make event invitations simple, beautiful and accessible.
- **Promise:** We turn your event details into a stunning digital experience your guests will love.
- **Vision:** The most trusted and preferred digital invitation platform for life's special moments across Ethiopia and beyond.
- **Values:** Creativity · Innovation · Client Focus · Quality · Trust · Excellence
- **Personality:** Creative · Modern · Friendly · Reliable · Professional · Inspiring
- **Symbol:** Mobile phone + quill. Phone = digital world; quill = creativity and personal touch.

## Colors
| Token | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--purple-600` | Primary Purple | `#6D28D9` | Buttons, links, key accents |
| `--purple-400` | Secondary Purple | `#A78BFA` | Hover, secondary accents, gradient end |
| `--lavender-100` | Accent Lavender | `#EDE9FE` | Section backgrounds, cards, chips |
| `--navy-900` | Deep Purple / Navy | `#24104F` | Headings, dark sections, footer |
| `--white` | White | `#FFFFFF` | Main background, text on dark |
| gradient | Brand gradient | `#6D28D9 → #A78BFA` | Hero/CTA band only |

**Usage ratio (suggested, deck chart not readable as text):** ~60% white/lavender, 20% navy, 15% primary purple, 5% secondary purple/gradient.

Text colors: body `#24104F` at ~80% on white; on dark use white. Check contrast ≥ 4.5:1.

Occasion accent colors are **not** brand colors. Do not add pink/gold UI chrome. Occasion variety comes from the *sample invitations' own artwork*, shown inside phone frames.

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Poppins — Bold / SemiBold
- **Body & UI:** Inter — Regular / Medium (Light allowed for captions)
- **Amharic:** Noto Sans Ethiopic (fallback, matches Inter's neutral feel)

| Style | Size / line-height | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | 48 / 56 | Bold |
| H2 | 32 / 40 | SemiBold |
| H3 | 24 / 32 | SemiBold |
| Body | 16 / 24 | Regular |
| Caption | 14 / 20 | Regular |
| Button | 16 / 24 | SemiBold |

Mobile: scale H1 to 36/44, H2 to 28/36. Never below 16px body.

## Layout
- 12-column grid; margins **60px desktop / 32px mobile**; gutter **20px desktop / 16px mobile**
- Rounded corners (suggest 16px cards, 999px buttons), soft shadows on cards only
- Layout types: Hero · Content · Split · Gallery

## Logo rules
- Use official files only. Variants: primary stacked, horizontal, symbol/icon, app icon; monochrome, reversed (on dark), grayscale.
- Clear space = height of the feather icon on all sides.
- Minimum size: 60px digital (25mm print).
- **Don't:** stretch, rotate, crop, recolor, add shadows/effects, place on busy images.
- Header: horizontal logo on white. Footer / dark sections: reversed version.

## Graphic elements
- Rounded shapes, soft containers, clean dividers
- Subtle feather pattern as background/overlay (very low opacity)
- Photos/mockups in rounded frames with a soft brand-shape overlay

## Photography
Real people, real moments, real emotions. Natural, bright, modern, emotional, elegant, clean. Natural light, uncluttered, sharp, high-res. Categories: Weddings, Graduations, Birthdays, Other (family, corporate).

## Icons
Minimal, rounded corners, consistent stroke weight, brand colors only. Filled (primary), outline (secondary), two-tone (accent). Library suggestion: Lucide (outline), restyled to brand stroke.
