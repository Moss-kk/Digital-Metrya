# 07 · Marketing and Launch Plan

## Goal
Get the first 20 paid invitations in 60 days and build a library of real examples.

## Pre-launch (2–3 weeks before)
- [ ] Reserve @digitalmetrya on Instagram, TikTok, Telegram (channel), Facebook, YouTube
- [ ] Profile, cover, bio use brand assets (slide 16 of deck)
- [ ] Teaser posts: "Something new for your celebration is coming"
- [ ] Offer 3–5 **founding customers** a discounted/free invitation in exchange for feedback + permission to show it
- [ ] Prepare 6 demo links and 6 short screen recordings (15–20 sec)
- [ ] Waitlist form on a "coming soon" page

## Content pillars
| Pillar | Example |
|---|---|
| Show the product | 15-sec phone screen recording of an invite opening |
| Before / after | Plain details text → finished invitation |
| Occasion spotlight | Weekly: wedding, birthday, graduation... |
| Tips | "7 things to put on a wedding invitation" |
| Behind the scenes | Designing the quill/phone symbol, building an invite |
| Customer stories | Real invites (with permission) |

Post templates exist in the brand deck (wedding, graduation, birthday, multi-event, Instagram stories, Save the Date).
Hashtags: `#DigitalMetrya #DigitalInvitations #YourEventYourWay`

## Channels (Ethiopia-first)
1. **Instagram** (Reels + Stories) — main showcase
2. **Telegram** — channel for samples, bot/DM for orders
3. **TikTok** — screen-recording and design process videos
4. **Facebook** — wedding and family audiences
5. **WhatsApp status/groups** — referrals
6. **Local partnerships** — photographers, wedding planners, event halls, printing shops, bakeries, MCs (give them a referral code)
7. **Print touchpoints** — QR on business cards, roll-up banner at wedding expos (brand deck slide 15)

Paid (after first 5 customers): Instagram/Facebook ads targeting engaged couples, graduating students (seasonal), expecting parents. Creative = the phone demo video + "See a sample" CTA.

## Seasonality (verify against your own calendar)
Plan campaigns ahead of wedding season, graduation season, and major holidays. Prepare content two to three weeks early.

## Referral loop built into the product
Every invitation footer carries a small "Made by Digital Metrya" link. Guests who love it become leads. Offer discount for referrals.

## Launch week checklist
- [ ] Site live on domain, SSL, analytics
- [ ] Test order form end to end, test Telegram alert
- [ ] Test on 3 phones (Android low-end, Android mid, iPhone) and slow network
- [ ] Share links preview correctly (OG image) on Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram
- [ ] Announce: Reel + Story + Telegram + WhatsApp status
- [ ] Personally message 30 people who might need an invitation soon
- [ ] Reply to every inquiry within 1 hour for the first month

## Metrics
| Metric | Target (month 1) |
|---|---|
| Visitors | 1,000 |
| Demo opens | 40% of visitors |
| Order form starts | 5% |
| Paid orders | 10–20 |
| Avg. response time | < 1 hour |
| Social followers | 500 |

## Feedback loop
After each delivered invite: ask for 1 testimonial, 1 photo/screenshot permission, 1 referral. Update samples and social proof monthly.

## Risks
- No real samples → fix first (Phase 0)
- Slow replies lose orders → set up Telegram alerts
- Pricing unclear → publish "From ___ ETB"
- Looks like every AI site → follow `04-design-direction.md`
