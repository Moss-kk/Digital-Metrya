# 02 · Website Strategy

## One-sentence pitch
We design beautiful digital invitations for any event and give you one link and one QR code to share with every guest.

## The 5-second test
A first-time visitor must understand, without scrolling:
1. **What:** a digital invitation (not a card printout, not an app)
2. **How they get it:** a link + QR code
3. **For which events:** wedding, birthday, graduation, book launch, baby shower, anything
4. **What to do next:** See samples / Order yours

The hero shows a **real invitation inside a phone**, the QR code, and a link bar. That single visual answers all four.

## Audience
| Who | Wants | Worry |
|---|---|---|
| Couples (25–35) | Elegant wedding invite, RSVP, location, share on Telegram/WhatsApp | Will it look cheap? Will older relatives open it? |
| Parents (baby shower, birthday, graduation) | Fast, pretty, easy | No time, not technical |
| Graduates / students | Affordable, shareable on social | Budget |
| Authors / businesses (book launch, corporate) | Professional, trackable | Brand fit |
| Event planners (later) | Repeatable, bulk | Turnaround |

Primary buyer decides on mobile, from a social post, in under a minute. Design for that.

## What the customer gets (use this list everywhere)
1. A custom-designed invitation page (not a template swap)
2. One short link (digitalmetrya.com/your-name)
3. A QR code for print, stories, and screens
4. Event details: date, time, venue, map, schedule
5. Photos and story (optional)
6. RSVP / guest response (package-dependent)
7. Works on every phone, no app to install
8. Revisions until you're happy (define count)

*Confirm each item is truly offered before it goes on the site.*

## Positioning
- **Not** a template marketplace. **Yes** a design service with a personal touch ("Our Digital Touch").
- Local advantage: Ethiopian dates and Amharic, Telebirr/CBE payment, local support on Telegram.
- Tone: warm, human, elegant. Avoids "tech startup" feel.

## Differentiators (pick the ones that are true)
- Amharic + English invitations, Ethiopian calendar support
- Delivered in [X] days
- One link works for everyone, no download
- QR code included for printed cards and venue screens
- Real designer, not an automated generator

## Occasions (launch)
Wedding · Birthday · Graduation · Baby Shower · Book Release · Custom ("Anything else")
Later: engagement, anniversary, house-warming, corporate, memorial, religious events.

## Offer structure (placeholder — set real prices in ETB)
| Package | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | Birthdays, small events | 1-page invite, link, QR, details + map |
| **Signature** | Weddings, graduations | Simple + gallery, story, RSVP, music, 2 revisions |
| **Custom** | Anything | Fully custom design, multi-page, guest list tools |

Price display rule: show "From ___ ETB". Hide nothing behind "contact us" — price clarity builds trust.

## Conversion goals
1. **Primary:** "Order your invitation" (opens order form or Telegram/WhatsApp)
2. **Secondary:** "View samples" (live demos)
3. **Tertiary:** follow on Instagram / Telegram channel

Target: ≥ 4% of visitors click an order button; ≥ 40% open at least one live demo.

## Trust builders
- Live demo invitations (open in-page)
- Real customer invites with permission (post-launch)
- Clear process: 4 steps
- Clear price
- Phone number + Telegram visible
- FAQ answering "What if guests have no smartphone?" (print the QR, send the link by SMS)

## Brand-driven risks to watch
- The brand is purple. Easy to look like every AI-built site. See `04-design-direction.md`.
